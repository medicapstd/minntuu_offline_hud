# 🤖 Jarvis Command Guide

Here is a complete list of all the voice and text commands you can use with your Jarvis assistant.

---

## 🗣️ Conversation & AI
*   **"Talk with me"** or **"Talk to me"**
    *   *Action:* Activates DeepSeek AI. Jarvis will ask you what you want to talk about, and you can have a full conversation with him.

## 🧠 Smart Features (New!)
*   **"Summarize my clipboard"** or **"Read clipboard"**
    *   *Action:* Takes whatever text you last copied (Ctrl+C / Cmd+C) and reads a 2-sentence AI summary of it out loud.
*   **"System status"**, **"CPU usage"**, or **"Check battery"**
    *   *Action:* Jarvis will check your Mac's internal sensors and tell you the current CPU load and Battery percentage.

## 💤 Wake & Sleep
*   **"Stop"**
    *   *Action:* Jarvis will stop listening to background noise and go to sleep.
*   **"Jarvis"**, **"Wake up"**, or **"Resume"**
    *   *Action:* Wakes Jarvis up from sleep mode so he can hear your commands again.
*   **"Exit"**
    *   *Action:* Completely closes the Jarvis application.

## 💻 App & File Management
*   **"Open [App/File Name]"** *(e.g., "Open Safari", "Open Notes")*
    *   *Action:* Jarvis will search your Applications folder and Documents folder to find and launch the app/file.
*   **"Close [App Name]"** or **"Quit [App Name]"** *(e.g., "Close Chrome")*
    *   *Action:* Jarvis will safely quit the specified running application.
*   **"Close background apps"** or **"Force quit apps"**
    *   *Action:* Jarvis will kill all non-essential background processes (keeping vital Mac services alive) to clear up system RAM and CPU.

## 🌐 Web Browsing
*   **"Open YouTube / Google / Gmail / ChatGPT"**
    *   *Action:* Instantly opens the specified website in your default browser.
*   **"Open [Website] in [Browser]"** *(e.g., "Open YouTube in Chrome")*
    *   *Action:* Opens the website in the specific browser you asked for (supports Safari, Chrome, Comet, ChatGPT Atlas).
*   **"New tab"**
    *   *Action:* Jarvis will detect which browser you are currently using and open a new blank tab for you. 

## ⚡ Mac Power Controls
*   **"Lock screen"**
    *   *Action:* Instantly locks your Mac.
*   **"Sleep"**
    *   *Action:* Puts your Mac to sleep.
*   **"Restart"**
    *   *Action:* Restarts your Mac.
*   **"Shutdown"**
    *   *Action:* Powers off your Mac.

---
*Tip: You can say these commands out loud using your microphone, or type them directly into the terminal where Jarvis is running!*
